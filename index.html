Fruit & Veggie Consumption

One way we can look at healthy eating across Australia is observing the population who are meeting the fruit and vegetable recommendations. Looking at Percentage of People Not Meeting Fruit and Vegetable Consumption Recommendations by State, we can observe that all the states of Australia have more than half their state population NOT meeting the consumption recommendations. Northern Territory have the worst statistics with 57.2 not meeting consumption where New South Wales has the best percentage with 52.2%.

Which states are the healthiest?

iSelect have come up with an index score from 0-10, to gauge Australia’s healthiest eaters. According to these scores, Northern Territory is the unhealthiest state with an index score of 3.43. Western Australia is recorded as the healthiest state with an index score of 6.58, Victoria has the second lowest index score of 4.29.

Another way we can observe which states are eating healthy is observing the google trend searches of each state. Values are calculated on a scale from 0 to 100, where 100 is the location with the most popularity as a fraction of total searches in that location. In this case Victoria has recorded the largest relative search interest with a score of 100. New South Wales and Queensland also showed high interest in health related searches with scores of 96 and 98 respectively. Again Northern Territory remained the lowest score, with a score of 71.
